The numbers

Measured, not estimated. The full ledger, including how each figure was taken and every caveat, is in STATS.md.

62 conformance checks per badge: 31 against a local backend, 31 against a real hosted deployment over TLS
22 languages recorded infeasible, with evidence, and replaced. Nothing was quietly dropped
355,755 lines of client and example source, as measured at the 83-language snapshot
50.67B tokens moved across the project, over 99% of them cheap cache reads
26 languages verified in a single 24-hour session: 57 to 83, with 489 commits and 80 pull requests merged
102 agents and workflows that reported in during that session
